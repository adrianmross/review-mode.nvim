Review Mode no longer loses what you typed or made. Opening another draft or closing the panel over a non-empty draft asks before discarding it, and when nobody can be asked (stopping the session, the panel window closing, `:q!` on the draft) the text goes to the unnamed register. Every failed post, reply, edit or queue from the draft leaves its text in the `"` register on GitHub, GitLab and local reviews alike, and queueing is no longer offered where it cannot work. In the review buffer `dd` confirms before dropping a pending comment and always drops the one under the cursor, and deleting the marker line no longer wipes the review body. Local review comments now live under `git rev-parse --git-common-dir`, so removing a worktree keeps them; they are keyed by the resolved branch with collision-free names, and existing stores are migrated. On-disk stores are written atomically, a store that cannot be parsed is set aside instead of being overwritten, and two Neovims reviewing different PRs no longer erase each other's viewed state. A review worktree holding commits of your own is never moved or cleaned, and reverting a trial suggestion you edited asks first.
